§ PRIMITIVES

Small model. Strange reach.

Three ideas compose into everything Legion does. No special cases in the router — participants are participants.

01 //

communicate is the only verb

Sync calls block until an answer arrives; fire-and-forget names a replyTo participant. Routing, sessions, delivery — all one mechanism.

02 //

Conversations are trees, not lists

Edit a message and re-run, prune a branch, compact a range — the full history stays recoverable in storage, always.

03 //

Authority fails closed

AuthEngine is a pure predicate. Anything requiring approval bubbles up the caller chain to an authorized approver or a boundary connector — or nothing happens.

04 //

Management is just tool calls

create_agent, retire_agent, policy edits — ordinary authorized tools over one API. No CRUD endpoints to secure separately.
