CONVERSATION TREE — GROWING
§1THE HUB

One verb: communicate

Every participant talks through the same call — synchronous, blocking until an answer, or fire-and-forget with a replyTo. There is no session layer, no special routing for humans. The router cannot tell your cursor from an agent's loop.

marginalia: the feed on the right uses this exact primitive — everything you saw is one mechanism.
§2MEMORY SHAPE

Conversations are trees

Messages link by parentId; the active chain is walked from the head. Edit and re-run forks a branch. Prune excludes without destroying. Compact summarizes a range. History is never lost — it grows.

watch the tree under the feed: forks are edits, dissolves are prunes.
§3FAILURE POSTURE

Authority fails closed

AuthEngine is a pure predicate: auto, requires_approval, or hidden. Requests needing authority bubble up the caller chain to an authorized approver — possibly a human at a boundary connector — or nothing happens.

that approval card in the rail? it bubbled all the way to your browser. deny it and watch the collective adapt.
§4ADMINISTRATION

Management is just tool calls

create_agent, retire_agent, policy edits — ordinary authorized tools over one gateway. No CRUD surface to secure, no second permission system to drift out of sync.
