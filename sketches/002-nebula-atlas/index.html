THE MODEL

Three primitives. Everything else follows.

No participant types get special treatment in the routing layer. One message hub, one policy engine, one conversation model — composed.

✉

communicate is the hub

Sync calls that block for an answer, or fire-and-forget with replyTo. Every participant speaks the same protocol.

⑂

Conversations are trees

Branch, edit, re-run, prune, compact. History is never lost — every node stays recoverable.

⛨

Auth that fails closed

Policy is a pure predicate. Approvals bubble up the call chain to someone authorized — or nothing happens.
